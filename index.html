About Rutuja

Natural henna.
No shortcuts.


Rutuja has been designing mehandi for brides and families across Warud for over five years. Every design is drawn freehand using natural, skin-safe henna — no black henna, no chemicals.

Each booking starts with a short conversation about your occasion, outfit, and how dark you want the stain. Then the design is built around that — not picked from a catalogue.
